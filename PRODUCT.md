# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary decision maker on the public landing pages: parents of youth football players. They compare training plans and prices, then contact the academy; the player is the motivation, the parent decides and pays. Inside the app: trainees, trainers, and admins (see CLAUDE.md).

## Product Purpose

Garden of Eden (גארדן אוף עדן) is a boutique football academy with two branches, חיפה and קריית אתא. The public site sells training plans; the platform runs assessments, progress tracking, nutrition, scheduling, and payments.

## Positioning

Football training combined with strength and athletics, plus a nutritional, mental, and tactical support layer, at a boutique field. Every session includes a ball.

## Operating Context

- Haifa does not sell online. Its price section leads to WhatsApp (972525779446); a phone call (052-5779446) is the secondary path. Address: שלמה המלך 57, חיפה.
- קריית אתא sells online through /join, fed by plan_products.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Hebrew only, RTL, logical CSS properties.
- Haifa price list (from the branch poster): 2 sessions a week 1,450 ILS a month; 1 session a week 850 ILS a month; intro track 2 sessions 200 ILS; 10-session card 2,100 ILS with a wide time limit; unlimited 1,650 ILS a month.

## Brand Commitments

- Name and logo: גארדן אוף EDEN, BOUTIQUE SOCCER FIELD (`public/logo-transparent.png`).
- The Haifa branch poster (dark field, neon green glow, gold for the unlimited plan) is a published brand asset the owner wants the site to echo.
- Site landing world in code: paper #F5F5F0, ink #1A1A1A, lime #CDEA68, Heebo.
- No emojis in UI or copy.

## Evidence on Hand

Real facility and training photos in `public/landing/` (athletic.webp, agility.webp, hero.webp and others), staff photos in `public/landing/staff/`, a promo video. No published testimonials with names or numbers beyond what the Testimonials section already holds; do not invent results, trainee counts, or awards.

## Product Principles

1. Prices are stated plainly and match the branch's published list.
2. Every price surface ends in one obvious way to talk to a person.
3. Show the real field and the real coaches rather than stock imagery.
